04 — BEFORE YOU ASK

A few useful
answers.

Do I need previous driving experience?

No. Let the school know whether you are new to driving or have driven before, so you can discuss the right place to begin.

What should I bring?

Ask the school which identification and documents you should bring before your first lesson.

How much does training cost?

Get in touch for the current training fees and a breakdown of what is included.

How do I get started?

Send an enquiry with a little information about your experience. The school can explain the available options and how to register.
